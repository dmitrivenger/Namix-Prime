Our Flagship Product

Phoenix Swap

Phoenix Swap is Georgia's digital asset exchange, enabling individuals and businesses to buy and sell a range of digital assets using Georgian Lari through direct bank transfers.

Built and operated by Namix Prime LLC, the platform is currently in the process of obtaining authorization as a Virtual Asset Service Provider (VASP) under Georgian law — with mandatory KYC and AML procedures enforced throughout.

Phoenix Swap is non-custodial by design. We never hold your crypto assets; funds transfer directly between your bank account and your specified wallet address.

How Phoenix Swap Works

From registration to receipt — the entire process takes minutes, not days.

01

Identity Verification

Create Your Account

Register with your email address and complete our KYC verification process in under five minutes. Once approved, your account is ready to trade immediately.

02

Rate Lock & Confirmation

Place Your Order

Select your digital asset, choose your preferred network, enter the amount, and lock in a live exchange rate sourced from the National Bank of Georgia with a transparent spread.

03

Settlement & Delivery

Receive Your Assets

Send your GEL via bank transfer or deposit crypto to the provided address. Once confirmed, your assets arrive directly in your specified wallet or bank account — no intermediary holds them at any point.

The Operator

Namix Prime LLC

Namix Prime LLC is a fintech holding company incorporated in Tbilisi, Georgia. We are the legal and corporate entity behind Phoenix Swap, and are currently in the process of obtaining the regulatory authorizations required to operate as a Virtual Asset Service Provider.

Our team brings together expertise across financial technology, software engineering, regulatory compliance, and product design. We focus on building regulated, user-facing infrastructure that connects traditional banking with digital assets in Georgia and the wider Caucasus region.

We believe compliant infrastructure is the foundation of lasting adoption. Phoenix Swap was built from the ground up to meet Georgian regulatory requirements — not retrofitted to them.

Namix Prime LLC operates under Namex, our parent holding group. Beyond Phoenix Swap, the Namex group is active across a range of initiatives spanning Web3 development and charitable endeavours — reflecting our commitment to building technology that creates genuine and lasting value.
